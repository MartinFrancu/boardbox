# One Punch Man - Design notes

What the game should feel like, and the idea pool for getting there.
(U) = your idea, (C) = Claude's addition. Nothing here is a rule yet.

## Experience pillars
1. **Protector**: civilians are what you care about. Losing people hurts
   emotionally *and* mechanically.
2. **Builder**: your Association grows. Heroes you invested in become
   easier to call, and you have more ways to spend money.
3. **Combo**: the "they did it together" moment. Several heroes stacking
   attacks to drop a monster that none could kill alone.
4. **Pressure**: Saitama and the monsters are a clock. Act before they decide
   it for you.

## Idea pool

### A. Civilians = economy (Protector)
- (U) Money is tied to living civilians. Fewer people = tighter money.
- (C) Income each round = 1 per X living civilians (replaces end-of-encounter
  payday).
- (C) Evacuate: pay money to protect civilians from this round's toll.

### B. Building your hero roster (Builder)
- (U) A hero you hired before is cheaper or easier to get next time.
- (U) HQ flats: buy a flat, the hero lives there, easy to summon.
- (U) Pagers: pay extra, the hero can be called again later (e.g. next turn).
- (U) Emergency tower: building that keeps heroes ready to react.
- (U) The hero pool accumulates: +1 hero each turn instead of a fresh roster.
- (U) Each monster brings 1 random hero for free; you can buy more.
- (U) Bigger ladder with filler heroes; promoted heroes get cheaper access.
- (C) Loyalty boxes on hero cards: tick one each time you hire them. 3 ticks =
  joins HQ permanently (combines flats, pagers and discounts in one mechanic).
- (C) Spending tiers so money is not all-or-nothing:
  1 = pager, 2-3 = hire, 4-5 = flat, 6+ = tower upgrade.

### C. Cost vs. rank
- (U) Higher-ranked heroes cost more, but price grows slower than power.
- (C) Cost by class C1 / B2 / A3 / S4, while stencil holes roughly double
  per class.

### D. Hero risk (Protector + Pressure)
- (U) Heroes can be injured and go to the hospital (Mumen Rider style).
- (U) A monster may hit heroes who attacked it instead of people, so heroes
  shield civilians.
- (C) Toll goes to attackers first: each hero who attacked this round can
  absorb 1 toll and is injured (out next round).
- (C) Hospital: injured heroes skip a round; pay to heal faster.

### E. Support & combos (Combo)
- (U) More support options.
- (U) Attack bonuses and multiplying combos.
- (C) Hero tags (Psychic, Cyborg, Martial, Blade...). 2+ heroes of one tag
  attacking the same round = bonus (+1 hole each, or double the last).
- (C) Setup moves: mark cells; the next hero's hits on marked cells count double.
- (C) Support types: Shield (toll -), Scout (peek next monster),
  Medic (heal a hero), Boost (next attack +1 hole),
  Coordinator (another hero may act again).

### F. Win / motivation
- (U) Each player has their own city with civilians. Most civilians at the end
  wins. You don't want monsters out of control, but don't want to help the
  others too much.
- (U) Each player has associated heroes and wants them at the top of the
  ladder, while people survive.
- (C) Hybrid: everyone loses if total civilians fall below X; otherwise the
  player with most civilians (or best hero ranks) wins.
- (C) Saitama's rise: if Saitama climbs into A class, everyone loses
  ("the Association is useless").

### G. Scale
- (U) More monsters, more places (districts).

## Biggest open choice
Co-op vs. competitive (F). It changes how money, heroes and civilians are
owned, so it should be decided before the other picks.
